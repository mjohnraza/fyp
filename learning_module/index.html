Building Businesses Around Fundamental Human Needs

Every successful business starts by solving a problem. Technology and trends change, but many human needs remain constant. Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs provides a useful framework for understanding these fundamental needs—and why certain markets remain relevant across generations.

Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs

I

Physiological Needs

The basic requirements for survival: food, water, warmth, sleep, and air.

II

Safety Needs

The desire for security, stability, financial protection, health, and a safe environment.

III

Love & Belonging

The need for friendship, family, intimacy, community, and meaningful connection.

IV

Esteem

The desire for self-respect, achievement, recognition, confidence, status, and independence.

V

Self-Actualization

The desire to reach one's potential through personal growth, creativity, learning, and meaningful goals.

These fundamental needs create enduring markets such as wealth, health, and relationships— markets that remain relevant regardless of changing technology or trends.

Wealth — Safety & Esteem

People seek financial security, freedom, achievement, and a better standard of living.

Common problems include:

  • Earning more money
  • Building a business
  • Finding better career opportunities
  • Developing valuable skills
  • Achieving financial independence

The methods may change, but the desire for financial security and success remains.

Health — Physiological & Safety

People want to survive, feel healthy, have energy, and enjoy a higher quality of life.

Common problems include:

  • Diet and nutrition
  • Exercise and fitness
  • Sleep and recovery
  • Stress and mindfulness
  • Weight management

The products may change, but the desire to feel and live better remains.

Relationships — Love & Belonging

People want to love and be loved, belong to a community, and build meaningful connections.

Common problems include:

  • Recovering from breakups or divorce
  • Building meaningful relationships
  • Improving communication
  • Becoming more confident in dating
  • Finding community

Technology changes how people connect, but the underlying need for connection remains.

From Need to Business

Maslow helps us understand the fundamental need. Entrepreneurship begins when we identify a specific problem within that need and create an offer that delivers a valuable outcome.

Find an enduring problem, solve it better, and build an offer around the outcome people truly want.
