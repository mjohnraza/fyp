# Agentic Entrepreneur OS: Multi-Agent Orchestration Skill

This skill teaches the agent how to run, inspect, and optimize the **Agentic Entrepreneur OS** pipeline developed for FAST NUCES FYP-I (Session Fall 2026).

## Theoretical Grounding & Pillars
The system is anchored in 6 peer-reviewed economic and management foundations:
1. **Multiplicative Growth & Wealth Condensation** (Bouchaud & Mézard [11]) - Shifting from additive hourly labor to multiplicative scalable assets.
2. **Opportunity Surface Area** (Pluchino et al. [12]) - Broadening distribution hooks via zero-friction lead magnets.
3. **Sequential Decision-Making under Uncertainty** (Kelly [13]) - Downside-capped risk sizing for validation experiments.
4. **AI-Driven Economic Leverage** (Maresca [14]) - Capitalizing on model inference for non-linear execution.
5. **Resource-Based View of the Firm** (Barney [15]) - Defensibility derived from rare skill intersections and proprietary data.
6. **Theory of the Growth of the Firm** (Penrose [16]) - Internal asset accumulation and learning loops.

## Core Multi-Agent Workflow
1. **Profile Ingestion (Agent 1a)**: Converts unstructured developer experience into `Capability`, `Context`, and `Constraint` schemas.
2. **Capability Decomposition (Agents 1b, 2, 1d)**: Groups skills into cohesive clusters, mines high-leverage cross-cluster intersections, and audits feasibility contradictions.
3. **Market Evidence Retrieval (Agent 3)**: Queries cold-start corpus across 5 outcome categories (Workflow Automation, Data ETL, Knowledge Bases, Compliance, Dev Tooling).
4. **Triangulation Check**: Verifies that at least 2 distinct source types (Forums, Reviews, Job Postings, Industry Reports) corroborate the pain point.
5. **Obstacle-Chain Extraction (Agent 4)**: Maps friction across chronological buyer phases (`BEFORE_ATTEMPT`, `DURING_EXECUTION`, `AFTER_COMPLETION`).
6. **Dual-Graph Translation (Agent 5)**: Applies the **Ownership Mindset Reframing Pass** (Power 4) to formulate grounded buyer-relief statements.
7. **Value Equation Offer Diagnosis (Agent 8)**: Evaluates $V = \frac{\text{Dream} \times \text{Likelihood}}{\text{Time} \times \text{Effort}}$ and pinpoints offer bottlenecks.
8. **Product-Suite Generation (Agent 9)**: Generates 3-tier funnel: Free Lead Magnet, Core Offer ($1k-$3.5k), and Recurring Backend ($399-$599/mo).
9. **Ten-Dimension Scorecard (Agent 7)**: Populates Table III metrics (0-100 total).
10. **Human-in-the-Loop Gate & Regret-Minimization Memo**: Halts execution before external spending, framing validation through Power 1 (Regret Minimization) and Power 7 (Self-Permission).
